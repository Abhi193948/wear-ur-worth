For clients seeking feather-light weight combined with exceptional flexibility and complete odorlessness during application. 4. Nail Shapes, Lengths & Lifestyle Ergonomics

Selecting the ideal nail shape is both an aesthetic and biomechanical decision. The right silhouette accentuates your finger proportions, elongates the visual appearance of the hands, and withstands daily typing and manual activities effortlessly.

Almond Shape

Tapered sides that culminate in a soft, rounded peak. Highly flattering for all hand types, creating the optical illusion of slender, elongated fingers. Excellent structural strength due to the absence of sharp corners.

Best For: Daily office wear, bridal styling, medium-to-long extensions.

Coffin / Ballerina Shape

Tapered sides that terminate in a crisp, sharp straight horizontal edge, mimicking a ballerina's pointe shoe. Bold, dramatic, and a favorite of high-fashion runways and celebrity styling.

Best For: Long extensions, bold nail art, statement social events.

Classic Square & Squoval

Square features sharp, 90-degree parallel sidewalls and a straight free edge. Squoval softens the outer corners slightly, combining square strength with oval practicality to prevent snagging on knitwear.

Best For: Short-to-medium lengths, active sports, high-speed typing.

Oval & Rounded

Follows the natural contour of your fingertip with smooth, curved edges. Highly resilient against accidental impacts and impossible to snag or chip.

Best For: Natural nail overlays, short natural nails, healthcare workers.

Stiletto Shape

Dramatically tapered sidewalls ending in a razor-sharp, dramatic point. Unapologetically fierce and theatrical, stiletto nails are sculpted with a reinforced apex to maintain structural integrity.

Best For: Photo shoots, editorial modeling, bold statement glamour.

Custom Hybrid Shapes

Can't decide? During your personal consultation, Abhishek will evaluate your nail bed curvature, cuticle shape, and daily typing posture to sculpt a custom hybrid shape tailored exclusively to you.

Best For: Bespoke individuality and maximum comfort.

8. The 6-Week Longevity Secrets: Essential Aftercare

While our structural sculpting provides unmatched bond strength, maintaining flawless aesthetics and natural nail health requires mindful daily habits. Follow these golden rules:

1. Daily Cuticle Hydration

The secret to flexible, non-lifting nails is moisture. Apply nourishing cuticle oil (preferably cold-pressed jojoba or almond oil) twice daily. Hydrated natural nails flex naturally with your extensions instead of pulling away and creating air pockets.

2. Jewels, Not Tools

Never use your enhanced nail tips to open beverage cans, scrape labels, or pry battery covers. Apply pressure with the pads of your fingers or utilize a blunt object to protect your structural apex from sudden shear stress.

3. Shield Against Chemicals

Prolonged contact with hot water, bleach, detergents, and household cleaning chemicals can slowly break down gel polymers and dehydrate skin. Always wear protective rubber or silicone gloves when performing heavy household chores.

4. Timely 3-Week Infill

As your natural nail grows forward, the reinforced apex shifts forward toward the free edge, altering the center of balance and increasing strain on your natural nail bed. Schedule a gentle infill every 3 to 4 weeks to re-balance the apex.

5. Never Peel or Rip

If you experience minor lifting at a corner, resist the urge to peel or bite! Peeling forces off microscopic layers of your natural keratin plate, leading to thinning and sensitivity. Book a painless removal or apply a drop of adhesive temporarily.

6. Safe Soaking Removal

When you are ready to remove your extensions or press-ons, opt for a professional appointment or follow our gentle soak-off guide using warm water, soap, and cuticle oil for tabs, or pure acetone foil wraps for permanent acrylics.

Are wearyourworth custom press-on nails really reusable?

Yes! What is the difference between Acrylics, Hard Gel, and BIAB?

Acrylic extensions combine liquid monomer and polymer powder for unmatched structural rigidity and length. Hard gel is an oligomer formula that cures under UV/LED light, providing lightweight feel and natural flexibility. BIAB (Builder in a Bottle) is an ultra-nourishing soak-off builder gel specifically designed to strengthen and protect natural nails, allowing brittle nails to grow without artificial extensions.

We offer two simple methods: you can order our Physical Sizing Kit in your preferred shape to try on sample numbered tips at home, or you can place a strip of transparent tape across the widest part of your natural nail bed, mark the sidewalls, and measure the distance in millimeters against a ruler. When sculpted with our balanced apex architecture and non-acid primers, our salon extensions comfortably last between 4 and 6 weeks without chipping or lifting. We recommend scheduling a gentle maintenance infill around week 3 to re-balance the apex as your natural nails grow forward.

Never at wearyourworth. Natural nail health is our foundational priority. We never use aggressive acid primers or industrial MMA monomer, and our Russian manicures use ultra-gentle diamond hardware bits that remove only dead skin tissue without thinning the natural keratin plate.
